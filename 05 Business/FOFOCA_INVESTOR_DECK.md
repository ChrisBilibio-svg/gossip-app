### 1 · Title
**Fofoca — Brazil's gossip prediction game**
Curated rumors → crowd predicts 🍵 TEA / 🧢 CAP → credible sources resolve.
*Speaker:* A status game for people who are always "right first."

### 2 · The problem — *gossip attention is massive but fragmented*
- Fans chase rumors across Instagram, X, blogs, TV, group chats — nothing ties it together.
- "Rumor" vs "confirmed fact" is blurry → erodes trust, fuels misinformation.
- Reading gossip is passive; nothing rewards being early or right.
- No scoreboard for people whose instincts are consistently correct.

### 3 · The insight — *the truth filter can become the game*
- Every Speculated rumor is an open prediction.
- The crowd expresses probabilities, not facts (TEA = true, CAP = false).
- When evidence lands, correct early callers earn points, rank, status.
- Skill-weighted scoring rewards contrarian-and-correct, not loud.

### 4 · The product — *a daily loop around open bets*
- 1 + 6 curated daily format: one hero "Fofoca do Dia" + supporting rumors.
- Tap TEA/CAP, lock the call, reveal the crowd split.
- Article view: headline → summary → full story → comments.
- Resolution payoff: points, leaderboard movement, bragging rights.
- Anonymous-first: play instantly, save account later.

### 5 · How it works — *evidence-first, skill-weighted*
- Curators publish from free sources (AI-summarized drafts, human-approved).
- Source credibility tagged: reliable → Confirmed, social → Speculated.
- Resolves on 2+ credible sources, or an explicit deadline.
- Money never touches the truth meter.

### 6 · Why now — *Brazil is primed*
- Mobile-native celebrity/fandom culture at scale.
- "Palpite"/betting behavior makes prediction intuitive.
- AI makes summarization/curation cheap; humans keep legal judgment.
- No incumbent owns "the gossip scoreboard."

### 7 · Market & wedge — *start narrow*
- Beachhead: BBB/reality TV, celebrity couples, football transfers, or a creator ecosystem.
- Win the daily habit in one wedge first.
- Expansion: entertainment → sports rumors → viral news.
- Cold-start is the central risk → launch where the crowd already gathers.

### 8 · Business model — *don't bet the company on ads*
- Free core game (liquidity + habit) — never charge to predict.
- Light ads after retention — secondary.
- Pro R$ 19.90/mo — alerts, stats, status cosmetics; never pay-to-win.
- Gossip Pulse (B2B) — anonymized belief/trend dashboards.

### 9 · Traction — *v1 code-complete enough to test*
- Expo + Supabase: feed, betting, evidence-first resolution, leaderboard, comments, admin.
- Content automation drafts curator-ready rumors from free sources.
- Source-credibility tagging, prediction deadlines, keyword search shipped.
- Next gap is launch + analytics + legal, not code.
