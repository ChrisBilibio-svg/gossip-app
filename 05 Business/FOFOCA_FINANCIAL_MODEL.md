# Fofoca Financial Model — v0.1

**Purpose:** realistic founder/investor planning model for the Brazil-first gossip prediction game. This is not a forecast; it is a set of explicit assumptions we can defend, test, and revise after beta data.

**Files:**
- `05 Business/fofoca_financial_model.xlsx` — 36-month scenario workbook.
- `05 Business/fofoca_financial_model_base.csv` — quick base-case monthly export.

## Core model choice

Do **not** pitch Fofoca as an ads-only consumer app. Brazil social eCPMs are too thin early. The realistic revenue stack is:

1. **Free core prediction game** — this drives habit and data; never charge for betting.
2. **Light ads after retention** — modest contribution, not the company thesis.
3. **Pro subscription** — speed/status/flex: early alerts, advanced stats, profile flair, custom persona cosmetics. Money never improves truth or scoring.
4. **Gossip Pulse B2B** — aggregate trend/sentiment dashboard for media, creators, journalists, agencies, and entertainment teams once audience scale exists.

- Month 36 base case reaches **316,759 MAU**, **79,190 DAU**, **R$ 176,243/mo** revenue, and **R$ 107,742/mo** net.

## Investor-level milestones

### Phase 1 — Validation, months 1–6
- Goal: prove the habit, not revenue.
- Ship Android-first beta, daily curated feed, betting, payoff, leaderboard, moderation.
- KPI gates: activation ≥ 45% of new users place a bet; D1 prediction retention ≥ 35%; D7 prediction retention ≥ 15–20%; ≥ 2.5 bets/DAU/day.
- Spend: product contractors, curation, legal/trust, lightweight community seeding.

### Phase 2 — Growth, months 7–18
- Add notifications, creator/fandom partnerships, better personalization, source/evidence tooling.
- Start ads carefully; introduce Pro only after users already love the game.
- KPI gates: DAU/MAU ≥ 25%; ≥ 3 bets/DAU/day; median open bets/user > 1; complaint/report rate controlled.

### Phase 3 — Monetization, months 15–36
- Launch Gossip Pulse as a paid dashboard once data density exists.
- Add B2B pilots: weekly belief reports, reality-show/fandom dashboards, influencer/agency watchlists.
- Ads + Pro support the consumer product; B2B is the margin expansion path.

## Hard realism / risks

- **Cold start is the biggest risk.** A prediction market with no crowd is dead. Launch should target a dense beachhead: one reality show, one celebrity cluster, football transfer rumor season, or a creator-led gossip community.
- **Legal/moderation is non-negotiable.** Copy must stay opinion/probability framed. User-submitted rumors remain out until trust tooling is stronger.
- **Resolution latency can kill retention.** Open loops are good; unresolved stale loops are bad. Explicit deadlines and evidence-first resolution are necessary.
- **Ads will not save the business early.** The model treats ads as a secondary line.
- **Pro must be status/speed, not pay-to-win.** Money never touches scoring or truth.
