# Fofoca Pitch Deck — v0.1

## 1. Fofoca
**Brazil’s gossip prediction game**

- Curated rumors → crowd predicts TEA/CAP → credible sources resolve
- Anonymous status game for people who are always “right first”
- Free core loop; monetize speed, status, and aggregate trend data

## 2. The problem
**Brazilian gossip attention is massive but fragmented**

- Fans chase rumors across Instagram, X, blogs, TV, and group chats
- Rumor vs confirmed fact is blurry, reducing trust
- Reading gossip is passive; nothing rewards being early or right

## 3. The insight
**The truth filter can become the game**

- Every Speculated rumor is an open prediction
- The crowd does not claim facts; it expresses probabilities
- When evidence lands, correct callers get status and points

## 4. The product
**A daily loop built around open bets**

- 1+6 curated daily format: one hero “Fofoca do Dia” plus supporting rumors
- Tap 🍵 TEA or 🧢 CAP, lock the call, reveal the crowd split
- Resolution payoff: points, rank movement, and bragging rights

## 5. Why now
**Brazil is primed for this mechanic**

- Mobile-native celebrity/fandom culture
- Palpite/betting behavior makes prediction intuitive
- AI makes summaries and curation leverage cheap, but humans keep legal judgment

## 6. Market wedge
**Start narrow, become the default gossip game**

- Beachhead: dense communities around reality shows, celebrity couples, football transfers, or creator ecosystems
- Win on daily habit before broad aggregation
- Expansion path: broader entertainment, sports rumors, viral news

## 7. Business model
**Do not bet the company on ads**

- Free core game: maximum liquidity and habit
- Pro: early alerts, advanced stats, custom persona/status cosmetics
- Gossip Pulse: anonymized aggregate belief/trend dashboard for media and creators

## 8. Traction / build status
**The v1 product is already code-complete enough to test**

- Expo + Supabase app with feed, bets, resolution, leaderboard, comments, admin
- Content automation creates curator drafts from free sources
- Next gap is not code; it is beachhead launch + analytics + legal polish

## 9. Financial model
**Base case is lean, not fantasy viral**

- M12: 21,191 MAU; M36: 316,759 MAU
- M36 revenue: R$ 176,243/mo; M36 net: R$ 107,742/mo
- Base-case 36-month cash need: R$ 508,908 / US$ 96,020

## 10. Moat
**The defensibility compounds after traction**

- Resolved prediction history creates a reputation graph
- Crowd belief data becomes proprietary once dense
- Brand + moderation trust matters in a legally sensitive category

## 11. Risks
**Known risks and how we reduce them**

- Cold start: launch into one dense fandom/event, not all Brazil at once
- Legal: opinion-data framing, curator-only supply, moderation, LGPD
- Stale rumors: explicit deadlines and evidence-first resolution

## 12. The ask
**Fund the validation sprint**

- Goal: prove D1/D7 prediction retention and daily betting habit
- Use funds for product polish, legal/trust, curation, beachhead marketing, analytics
- Raise after proof: growth round to scale community + Gossip Pulse
