## 1. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (the
"Discloser") to the other (the "Recipient"), in any form (oral, written, electronic, visual),
whether or not marked "confidential", including but not limited to: source code, product
designs, the financial model and projections, cap table and fundraising plans, user and
usage data, business and marketing strategy, curation and moderation methods, pricing,
roadmaps, and the existence and content of discussions between the Parties.

## 2. Exclusions
Confidential Information does **not** include information that the Recipient can demonstrate:
(a) was public at the time of disclosure, or later became public through no fault of the
Recipient; (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient without obligation of confidence before
disclosure; (c) was independently developed without use of the Confidential Information; or
(d) was lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

## 3. Obligations of the Recipient
The Recipient shall:
- use the Confidential Information **solely** for the Purpose;
- not disclose it to any third party except to its employees, advisors, or agents who need to
  know it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as
  protective as this Agreement;
- protect it with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential
  information, and no less than reasonable care;
- not reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble any disclosed software or prototypes.

## 4. Compelled disclosure
If the Recipient is legally compelled (by court or authority) to disclose Confidential
Information, it shall, where lawful, give prompt notice to the Discloser and disclose only the
portion legally required.

## 5. Personal data (LGPD)
If any Confidential Information includes personal data, the Recipient shall process it only as
necessary for the Purpose and in compliance with the **LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018)**, and shall not
retain it beyond what the Purpose requires. `[Add a data-processing addendum if data is shared.]`

## 6. No license / no obligation
No license or intellectual-property right is granted by disclosure. Nothing in this Agreement
obligates either Party to proceed with any transaction or relationship.

## 7. Term & survival
- This Agreement is effective from the date above and continues for **`[2]` years**, unless
  superseded by a definitive agreement.
- Confidentiality obligations survive termination for **`[3]` years** after disclosure (or
  indefinitely for trade secrets, to the extent permitted by law).

## 8. Return or destruction
Upon written request or termination, the Recipient shall return or destroy all Confidential
Information and copies, and confirm destruction in writing, except one archival copy retained
for legal-compliance purposes only.

## 9. No warranty
Confidential Information is provided "as is", without warranty of accuracy or completeness.
