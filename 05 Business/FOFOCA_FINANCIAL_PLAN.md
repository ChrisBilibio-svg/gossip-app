# Fofoca — Financial Plan

**Purpose:** a realistic founder/investor planning model for the Brazil-first gossip
prediction game. This is **not a forecast or a guarantee** — it is a set of explicit,
defensible assumptions we can test and revise once beta data exists.

**Companion workbook:** `FOFOCA_FINANCIAL_PLAN.xlsx` (8 tabs)

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** Planning figures only. Not financial advice, not an offer to sell
> securities, and not a promise of returns. Valuation figures are illustrative.
> FX at R$ 5.30 / US$ 1.00.

---

## 1. Core revenue thesis

Do **not** pitch Fofoca as an ads-only consumer app — Brazilian social eCPMs are too thin
early. The realistic revenue stack, in order of importance:

1. **Free core prediction game** — drives habit and data. We *never* charge for predicting.
2. **Light ads after retention** — a secondary line, not the company thesis.
3. **Pro subscription (R$ 19.90/mo)** — speed & status: early alerts, advanced stats,
   profile flair, cosmetics. **Money never improves truth or scoring.**
4. **Gossip Pulse (B2B)** — anonymized belief/trend dashboards for media, creators,
   agencies, and entertainment teams, once audience scale exists.

---

## 2. Two strategic paths (the key decision)

The model runs the same business two ways — the real fork in front of the company:

### 🅐 Bootstrapped — *keep control, grow on cash flow*
- Hire only as profit allows; founders retain **~90%** (10% option pool).
- Follows the **organic / Base** growth curve.
- **M36:** 133,081 MAU · R$ 111.6k/mo revenue · **+R$ 36.5k/mo net profit** · team of 7.
- **Catch:** still needs **~R$ 752k** of early runway (founder savings / friends-family /
  a small angel) to survive the trough before turning cash-flow positive. Bootstrapping is
  *not* free — it trades dilution for personal/early capital risk.

### 🅑 Funded — *trade equity for speed & scale*
- Raise **Seed R$ 1.5M** (M7) + **Series A R$ 8.0M** (M24) = **R$ 9.5M** total.
- Capital funds an aggressive hiring ramp and paid growth → **capital-accelerated / Upside** curve.
- **M36:** 889,798 MAU · R$ 702.6k/mo revenue · **+R$ 422.1k/mo net** · team of 10.
- **Cost:** founders diluted to **~57.6%** after both rounds.

## 7. Milestones (path-agnostic)

**Phase 1 — Validation (M1–6):** prove the habit, not revenue. Android beta, daily curated
feed, betting, payoff, leaderboard, moderation. Gates: ≥45% of new users place a bet;
D1 prediction retention ≥35%; D7 ≥15–20%; ≥2.5 bets/DAU/day.

**Phase 2 — Growth (M7–18):** notifications, creator/fandom partnerships, personalization,
evidence tooling. Start ads carefully; introduce Pro only after users love the game.
Gates: DAU/MAU ≥25%; ≥3 bets/DAU/day; controlled report rate.

**Phase 3 — Monetization (M15–36):** launch Gossip Pulse B2B; weekly belief reports,
reality-show/fandom dashboards, agency watchlists. Ads + Pro support the consumer product;
B2B is the margin-expansion path.

## 8. Hard realism / risks

- **Cold start is the #1 risk.** A prediction market with no crowd is dead. Launch into a
  **dense beachhead** — one reality show (BBB), a celebrity cluster, football-transfer season,
  or a single creator-led gossip community.
- **Legal/moderation is non-negotiable.** Copy stays opinion/probability-framed.
  User-submitted rumors stay out until trust tooling is stronger. (See T&C + NDA drafts.)
- **Resolution latency can kill retention.** Open loops are good; *stale unresolved* loops are
  bad. Evidence-first resolution + explicit deadlines are mandatory.
- **Ads won't save the business early.** Modeled as a secondary line throughout.
- **Pro must be status/speed, never pay-to-win.** Money never touches scoring or truth.
- **Multiples are not promises.** 6× ARR is a planning convention; real value depends on
  growth durability, retention, and B2B traction.
