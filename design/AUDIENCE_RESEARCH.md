# Viddi — Audience Research (Brazil)

_In-depth audience/market research for Viddi, a Brazil-first gossip prediction game (free, play-money, anonymous-first, pt-BR). Two research passes, ~13 web searches, no multi-agent fan-out. Confidence tagged per figure; "verified" = adversarially fact-checked. Last updated 2026-06-18._

---

## ⚠️ Read first: the "prediction market" framing is now legally radioactive in Brazil

In **April 2026, Brazil banned prediction markets.** The National Monetary Council (CMN) passed a resolution declaring prediction-market derivatives illegal, and Anatel **blocked Kalshi, Polymarket + 27 platforms** ([iGaming Business](https://igamingbusiness.com/legal-compliance/compliance/brazil-prediction-markets-illegal/), [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2026/04/275471-brazilian-authorities-block-prediction-markets-kalshi-and-polymarket-to-ensure-investor-protection/)). The resolution explicitly excludes "events of a **political, electoral, social, cultural, entertainment, or any other nature**" as valid derivative underlyings — i.e. exactly gossip/pop-culture markets.

**Implications for Viddi:**
- **Viddi is almost certainly legal** — it's **free, play-money, no cash-out**, so it's a *game*, not a derivative or a bet. That's the shield.
- **But the label "mercado de previsões / prediction market" is now toxic in BR** — regulators, App-Store reviewers, press, and users will tie it to the banned platforms. **Position as "jogo de palpites" (a game), never "mercado"/"aposta."** Keep the sleek fintech *chrome*, drop the fintech *vocabulary* (BR-specific). The "money never touches truth/scoring" rule is now a compliance necessity, not just ethics.
- It also **removes a would-be competitor category** (no legal Polymarket/Kalshi in BR) — Viddi as a free game has the lane to itself.

Sources: [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/brazil-prediction-market-ban/), [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/fraud-and-fincrime/news/brazil-bans-prediction-markets-and-blocks-27-platforms-including-polymarket-and-kalshi).

## Market foundation (TAM context)

Brazil end-2025: **185M internet users (86.9% penetration)**, **150M social identities**, **TikTok 131M (18+)**, WhatsApp near-universal ("disguised social network"), Gen-Z Instagram-Stories + TikTok >80% ([DataReportal Digital 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-brazil)). _High confidence._ → Distribution is mobile-first, WhatsApp/IG/TikTok, group-chat-native.

## Segments (deep)

### 1. Reality-TV superfans (BBB / A Fazenda) — ⭐ beachhead
- **Size:** BBB reached **126.7M** Brazilians in 2025 (Kantar Ibope; reach, not fans); **#BBB25 = 37M tweets**, 41h world-trending; Globoplay +13% DAU during the season ([TELA VIVA](https://telaviva.com.br/22/04/2025/big-brother-brasil-alcancou-1267-milhoes-de-brasileiros-de-13-de-janeiro-a-15-de-abril/), [Textual](https://www.textual.com.br/tv-e-twitter-o-match-perfeito-como-on-e-off-se-sobrepoe-na-audiencia-do-bbb/)). A Fazenda = #2 reality (5.8 pts, beats SBT; [Terra](https://www.terra.com.br/diversao/tv/audiencias-1809-forca-de-mulher-bate-novo-recorde-na-record-e-a-fazenda-2024-vence-o-sbt,928cb3ea52af27cb5a85f24fdd1f5190vv1sca8r.html)). _Reach high-confidence; fan subset a proxy._
- **Psychographics:** competitive opinion-having ("eu falei que ela ia sair"), belonging, live FOMO. Skews female + Gen-Z/millennial. Purest product fit — a live season is a machine that emits daily resolvable markets (paredão, romances, brigas).
- **Channels:** X/Twitter (91% treat it as a TV complement), reality-recap creators, WhatsApp/Telegram fan groups. **Partner targets:** 10–100k reality-analyst/fofoqueira accounts, not just megas.

### 2. Celebrity-gossip followers
- **Size:** multiple pages 20M+ — Choquei ~20–27M (before its 2025 takedown), **Alfinetei ~25M (#46 most-followed BR account)**, Babadeira 2.7M ([Gazeta SP](https://www.gazetasp.com.br/entretenimento/choquei-o-maior-perfil-de-fofoca-do-instagram-some-sem-explicacao/1172904/), [oficinadanet](https://www.oficinadanet.com.br/post/19181-10-perfis-mais-seguidos-no-instagram-no-brasil)). Combined gossip following = tens of millions (heavy overlap). _Medium confidence._
- **Read:** massive *reach* but mostly **passive consumers** — Viddi converts them by turning passive scrolling into a call-and-score game. Reach via the pages themselves (publis) + shareable win cards.

### 3. Football "mercado da bola" / fantasy
- **Size:** **Cartola FC ~4M players, 1.7M DAU, 4.6M teams (2023)** ([Dicas Cartola](https://www.dicascartola.com.br/cartola-fc/tudo-sobre-o-cartola-fc/)); Rei do Pitaco **R$300M+** prizes paid. _Cartola solid; RDP prize ok, user counts dated (2022)._
- **Read:** already habituated to daily prediction games + status/competition; easiest "prediction-game" muscle memory. Male-skewed. Transfer-rumor markets ("mercado da bola") are a natural vertical beyond gossip.

### 4. Sports-betting-adjacent
- **Size:** **~24M** made Pix transfers to betting cos (BCB, Aug 2024, **verified**); 12% of Brazilians bet; **56% aged 16–39, 62% male; most 20–30yo** (DataSenado, **verified**) ([DataSenado](https://www12.senado.leg.br/radio/1/noticia/2024/10/01/pesquisa-do-datasenado-traca-perfil-dos-apostadores-esportivos), [BCB study](https://www.bcb.gov.br/conteudo/relatorioinflacao/EstudosEspeciais/EE119_Analise_tecnica_sobre_o_mercado_de_apostas_online_no_Brasil_e_o_perfil_dos_apostadores.pdf)).
- **Read:** fluent in odds/positions, but **62% male** clashes with gossip's female skew and they want real-money stakes Viddi won't offer. Secondary — and post-ban, keep distance from "aposta" vocabulary.

### 5. Gen-Z social/meme sharers
- The *distribution layer* across all segments (Stories/TikTok/group chats >80%). Low direct monetization, high K-factor. Design the win-card + share loop for them.

## Willingness to pay
Brazilians spend **~R$118/mo avg on digital subscriptions**; 48% plan to *increase* subscription spend by 2030; but the market is price-sensitive (all-streaming ≈ R$380 ≈ 23% of min wage) ([Mercado&Consumo](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/20/08/2025/servicos/servicos-de-streaming-lideram-modelo-de-assinaturas-no-brasil/), [TudoCelular](https://www.tudocelular.com/mercado/noticias/n235751/assinar-streaming-custa-380-reais-brasil-2025.html)). Viddi Pro at **R$19,90 ≈ 1.2% of min wage** is affordable but must feel clearly worth it. LatAm apps convert **~25% median trial→paid** _(top 10% 53%)_ with **late conversions (Week 6+)** ([RevenueCat 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/), **verified**) — a trial + patient nurture beats an aggressive upfront wall. Ads = secondary.

## Recommendation & 90-day playbook

**Beachhead: reality-TV superfans, launched on a live BBB or A Fazenda season.** Wins on reachability × engagement × monetization × fit: dense community, proven second-screen/group-chat habit, an event calendar that *manufactures* daily markets, and the female-skewing gossip audience the male betting segment doesn't cover.

1. **Pre-season (wks 1–4):** recruit **20–50 micro reality/fofoca creators (10–100k)** + a founders' Telegram ("Profeta Fundador" badge). Seed markets so the feed is never empty.
2. **Premiere-night launch (wk 5):** hero daily markets on the next eviction/romance/fight; one-tap "manda no grupo" WhatsApp share.
3. **Win-loop (ongoing):** the Stories win-card = the ad; every correct call is social proof.
4. **Ride trending windows:** a daily auto-generated "fechamento do dia" recap the recap-creators can repost.
5. **Retention:** weekly leaderboard reset (O Profeta) + streaks + tiers.

## Top risks
1. **Legal framing (new, critical):** post-ban, market as a **free game of palpites**, never a "mercado"/"aposta." Get BR counsel to bless the play-money positioning before launch.
2. **Fake-news / defamation (existential):** the Choquei / Jéssica Canedo case (false rumor → suicide → court-ordered takedown; [Correio](https://www.correio24horas.com.br/brasil/fake-news-e-morte-relembre-episodios-em-que-a-choquei-virou-caso-de-policia-0426), [CNN Brasil](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/nacional/choquei-admite-ter-divulgado-noticia-falsa-diz-que-apoiara-familia-de-jovem-morta-e-que-reavaliara-seus-metodos/)) is the trap. Viddi's **"opinião, nunca acusação" + sourced/deadline resolution** is both compliance and differentiator ("fofoca com recibo").
3. **Platform dependency** (Choquei's IG vanished overnight) + **dated fantasy sizing**.
