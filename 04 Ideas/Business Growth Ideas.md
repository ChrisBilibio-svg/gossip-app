# Business Growth Ideas — AI Vault Service

> Expanded from the May 26 build session. These are actionable ideas for growing the business from Phase 1 into something scalable.

---

## The Core Offering (recap)
Managed AI-powered organization system for executives and business owners.
They submit chaos. You return clarity — calendar, tasks, pipeline, next actions.
They never touch the tech. You operate it using Obsidian + Claude Code.

---

## Growth Ideas

### 1. Nail the Niche First
Don't try to serve everyone. Pick one type of client to start:
- **Real estate agents** — chaotic schedules, lots of leads to track, high income
- **Business coaches** — already value productivity systems, easy to upsell
- **Freelancers / consultants** — overwhelmed with admin, want to focus on work
- **Small business owners (2–10 employees)** — no EA, need structure badly

**Action:** Decide on one niche before your 3rd client. Go deep, not wide.

---

### 2. Pilot Month Offer
Lower the barrier for first clients:
> *"First 30 days at $150. If you love it, $399/month after that. Cancel anytime."*

This removes the risk for them. You get a real use case, a testimonial, and recurring revenue.

---

### 3. Pricing Tiers (suggested)
| Tier | Price/month | What's included |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $199 | Daily note + weekly summary email |
| Pro | $399 | Daily briefing + inbox processing + pipeline updates |
| Executive | $699 | Everything + weekly strategy call + on-demand updates |

Start with Pro as your flagship. Starter gets them in the door. Executive is the upsell.

---

### 4. Your Own Vault Is the Demo
You built this system today in one session. **Record it.**
- 2-minute screen recording of the morning briefing email arriving
- Show the vault structure in Obsidian
- Narrate what the client would experience

This is your sales asset. Show it on LinkedIn, in DMs, on a landing page.

---

### 5. LinkedIn Content Strategy
Post once a week. Topics that attract your ideal client:
- "I built an AI assistant for myself in one day — here's what it does"
- "Why executives keep failing at productivity tools (and what actually works)"
- "Before and after: what a chaotic inbox looks like vs. a structured vault"
- "The one thing your calendar app can't do"

**Rule:** Don't sell. Educate and show. The DMs will come.

---

### 6. Partnership Play
Find people who already have your clients:
- **Business coaches** — they have clients who need this. Offer a referral fee (10–20%).
- **Executive assistants** — they want AI tools to make their job easier. Partner with them.
- **Accountants / bookkeepers** — serve the same small business owner. Cross-refer.

One good partnership = 5–10 clients without cold outreach.

---

### 7. Cold Outreach (Simple Version)
LinkedIn DM template:
> *"Hey [Name] — I noticed you run [Company]. I build and manage custom AI organization systems for business owners — basically a smart assistant that structures their schedule, tasks, and pipeline daily, without them needing to learn any tool. Would it be worth a 15-min call to see if it fits what you're working on?"*

Send 5 of these a day. That's 150/month. Even a 2% reply rate = 3 conversations.

---

### 8. Build a One-Page PDF
One page. Clean. Three sections:
1. **The problem** — you're disorganized and existing tools require too much from you
2. **The solution** — I build and run it for you
3. **What you get** — daily briefing, structured vault, pipeline visibility, peace of mind

Price + a "book a call" link at the bottom. Send this to every lead.

---

### 9. Case Study — Use Yourself
Write up what you built today as a case study:
> *"How I built a personal AI assistant in one day — and what it would cost a business owner to have me do it for them."*

Post it on LinkedIn. Turn it into a blog post. It proves you can do it.

---

### 10. Referral Loop
After every client hits 30 days, ask:
> *"Is there one other business owner in your network who you think would benefit from this?"*

One referral per client compounding = fastest growth path for a service business.

---

### 11. Future: White-Label for VAs
Longer term — teach virtual assistants to use your system and charge them a licensing fee.
They bring the clients. You provide the framework and Claude Code infrastructure.
This is Phase 3 thinking — don't build it yet.
