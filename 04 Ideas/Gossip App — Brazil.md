# Gossip App — Brazil

**Status:** Raw Idea
**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Market:** Brazil (initial)

---

## Core Concept

An anonymous gossip aggregator and social platform, starting with Brazil. Pulls gossip from all major sources in one place, then lets users react and discuss — completely anonymous.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Gossip Aggregator
- Scrapes and pulls from Brazilian gossip sites, Instagram pages, Twitter/X accounts, celebrity pages, etc.
- Displays all content in a clean, structured feed — not raw links, but summaries and organized cards
- AI-generated summary layer so users can catch up fast

### 2. Confirmed vs. Speculated — Truth Filter ⭐
- Every piece of gossip is tagged: **Confirmed** or **Speculated**
- Separate sections/tabs in the feed so users can choose what they're browsing
- Confirmed = verified by multiple sources or official statement
- Speculated = rumor, unverified, anonymous tip
- Removes the fake news problem and builds trust in the app — users know what's real and what's not

### 3. Personalized Filters & Alerts ⭐
- Filter the feed by specific people, topics, or celebrities you care about
- Follow specific influencers, public figures, athletes, etc.
- Set **push notifications** for the people you follow — the moment gossip drops about them, you get pinged
- Basically a gossip alert system on the people that matter to you

### 4. Social Layer (Twitter-style)
- Users can view aggregated gossip and post their own reactions/opinions
- Comment threads on each gossip item
- Users can review/rate the credibility of gossip ("true tea" vs "cap")
- Posts and comments from the community

### 5. Full Anonymity
- Every account is anonymous — no real names, no profile linking
- Similar model to **Fizz** or **Yik Yak**
- Users can be consistent personas without revealing identity
