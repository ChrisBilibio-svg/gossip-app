# AI Vault Service — Core Idea

## The Concept
A **managed AI-powered personal assistant service** using Obsidian + Claude Code as the engine. You are the operator. Businesses are the clients. They don't touch the tech — they submit raw input and receive organized, structured output.

**You're not selling software. You're selling outcomes delivered through software you operate.**

---

## The Problem You're Solving
Executives and small business owners are chronically disorganized:
- Scheduling, follow-ups, and pipeline visibility are constant pain points
- Tools like Notion, Calendly, and Google Calendar exist but require them to learn and maintain the system
- Most don't. Or they start and abandon.

---

## The Solution
A "done for you" vault system:
1. Client submits raw input (email dump, voice memo, bullet points)
2. You (+ Claude Code) process it into: structured calendar, task list, pipeline, ideas
3. Client receives clarity — no learning required on their end

---

## What Makes It Different
- Not just a calendar app — it's a **linked thinking system**
- Human-curated + AI-assisted = better than either alone
- Custom structure per client, not a one-size-fits-all template
- Persistent and evolving — unlike a one-off ChatGPT query
